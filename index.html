Meet the Owner

We are Rated RV, a family from south Jersey — Sandi, Tim, Maddie, Piper and Eli. Sandi is a Registered Nurse who works in hospice care, a supreme party planner and decorator, and an amazing mom to her three kids. Tim is a stagehand and real estate professional with a penchant for renovation. Both have a passion for helping people, because a rising tide lifts all boats. Our journey has taken us from a tent, to a 5th wheel, to a Class A motorhome — from the campgrounds of New Jersey and Pennsylvania to the Grand Canyon.

We're here to share the world of RV'ing with you. Your trip can be far and wide, or just a few miles with family — the memories are what matter. We value the time spent outside together, and we want to help our clients enjoy the same. Got a question before you book? Email info@ratedrv.com and you'll hear back from the family that actually owns the RVs.

“Some folks will simply see a boy sitting by an old camper. I see freedom. I see turtles and frogs and catfish and sunnies. I was lucky enough to have these kinds of experiences shared with me as a kid, and now I'm doing the same with my kids. You can, too.”
